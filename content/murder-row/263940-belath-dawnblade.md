Not an enemy. Belath runs the **Illicit Rain**, and his spell list is the rulebook for the
tavern sequence rather than an ability set.

**Disguised** puts the party in employee clothing, and then four jobs are available: **Server**
(patrons order and lose patience), **Cleaner** (mop the spills), **Entertainer** (perform on
beat) and **Bouncer** (eject the rowdy ones).

has a card here because MDT places them on the map.
