A fight built on a trade the group has to keep making.

The [Ritual Snakes](#/d/altar-of-fangs/codex/mob/268358) beam Zul'jan for 8 seconds. Every beam that
lands grants **Fang Empowered** — 93k Nature per second to the whole group, stacking, which
is unsustainable. A player standing in the beam takes it instead for almost nothing, and picks
up **Ritual Venom**.

Ritual Venom is dormant for 50 seconds and then bursts for 387k. What clears it is
**Bloodletting**, which comes from his *Physical* abilities: taking his melee-range damage is
how the venom comes off.

So the loop runs body-block, carry the venom, stay in range of the damage that removes it. The
group that avoids everything is the group that explodes at 50 seconds.

**Chop Down** is two hits of 485k on the tank. **Axegrinder** ignores armour.
