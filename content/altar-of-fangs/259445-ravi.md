The fight is a feeding loop, and every part of it is written into the tooltips.

**Ravenous Stomp** breaks stalactites off the ceiling. Those rocks skewer the flying Twinfangs,
which drops **Fresh Meat** into the pit. When she next runs to a pile, a pile holding Fresh
Meat turns **Ssscavenging** into a **Feeding Frenzy** — the same all-damage-absorb shield at
the same Carrion Burst rate, with **Scent of Blood** on top for +30% attack speed. The frenzy
used to stack **Carrion Burst** twice as fast as a normal meal; it no longer does, so what
Fresh Meat costs the group is the attack speed, not the ramp. **Messy Eater** is not the
difference either — both tooltips throw chunks across the pit, one while Ssscavenging and one
while in a Feeding Frenzy.

While that shield is up she stacks **Carrion Burst** on the whole group every 3 seconds. The
shield is therefore a damage check with a timer attached, and the stomp is what sets its
difficulty.

**Hydrastrike** is the tank buster. **Regurgitate** is the one that punishes movement twice:
291k on the waves, then -50% speed on anyone who ate them.
