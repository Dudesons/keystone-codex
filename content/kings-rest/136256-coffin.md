The crypts [Mchimba](#/d/kings-rest/mob/134993) entombs players in. MDT places them as units
with 0.5 million health and **no spells at all**, which is exactly right — they do nothing,
they only have to be opened.

That health figure is the useful part of this card. Mchimba opens crypts at six seconds each
and deliberately starts with the empty ones; half a million health is considerably faster than
that.
