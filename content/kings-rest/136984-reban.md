The raptor the [Honored](#/d/kings-rest/mob/135192) and
[Skeletal](#/d/kings-rest/mob/137487) raptors are modelled on — their tooltips still carry his
name.

He is worth no forces and MDT lists no crowd control that applies to him, so the interrupt on
**Deathly Roar** is the only interaction available. A four-second fear is expensive anywhere;
in a dungeon full of Burning Ground and Purifying Flame it is worse than the 118k suggests.

The tooltip's own text says *"Rezan lets out a terrifying roar"* — Blizzard's copy, carried
through from the original King's Rest boss.
