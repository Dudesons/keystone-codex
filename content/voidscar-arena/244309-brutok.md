A single unit worth 25 forces, and the most rewarding mob in the dungeon to play well against.

**Smashing Charge** is 388k down a line. Avoid it and **Concussion** fires: Brutok stuns
himself for five seconds and takes 20% more Physical damage for the duration. So the dodge is
not merely damage avoided, it is a free burst window handed to the group — and a charge that
lands costs twice, once in health and once in the window not taken.

**Head Bash** deserves a flag: its spittle ticks *until cancelled*, with no listed duration,
and its damage figure in the data is **unscaled**. What it does is clear; what it costs is not.
