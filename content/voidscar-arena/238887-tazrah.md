A fight about the floor, with a scheduled reckoning.

**Void Fissure** accumulates during the fight; **Dark Bloom** then hits everyone for 175k *and*
erupts every fissure for a further 242k. The party-wide number is not the problem — the
interaction is. A group that has kept the room tidy takes one hit; a group that has not takes
several.

**Ethereal Shades** compound it by adding casters of **Umbral Rupture**, so the number of
10-yard rifts opening at once grows as the fight runs.

**Void Blast** is the tank hit, and its knockback is worth respecting for where it sends
people rather than for the 272k.
