A hundred and fifteen units at one force each — the filler of the Vale. Individually
irrelevant, and worth an entry only because there are so many of them.

**Spore Spines** does 1 damage a tick, so the number is not the point: it is a 2% slow that
stacks with every melee swing and holds for 16 seconds. Standing in a crowd of Lashers, a
melee player can find themselves meaningfully slowed with nothing on screen to explain it. It
carries a magic dispel type if it ever gets that far.

They are also what the [Radiant Spellsower](#/d/the-blinding-vale/codex/mob/245336) wakes up when its
channel goes through, which is the real reason to know they exist.
