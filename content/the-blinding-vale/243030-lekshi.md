The third of the **Lightblossom Trinity**, and the one that makes the fight move.

**Lightsower Dash** does 165k to anything in its line and plants a Lightblossom seed at each
end — the seeds Kezkitt then beams. What makes it manageable is that it does not pick a random
line: it dashes *between patches of Fertile Loam*, which Meittik created and everyone can see.
The path is therefore knowable before the cast finishes.

**Fan Of Thorns** is 194k a second for three seconds inside 15 yards, and **Thornblade** is a
12-second bleed on whoever it ambushes. Between the three bosses, the group is dodging, soaking
and dispelling at once — the shared health pool is the only thing that is simple here.
