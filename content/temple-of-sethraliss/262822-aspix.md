The caster half of the pair, fought with [Adderis](#/d/temple-of-sethraliss/codex/mob/262530).

Two things make this fight about pacing rather than damage. **Storm Blessed** moves an immunity
back and forth, so the group's damage has to follow it. And **Frenzy** punishes finishing one
before the other — the survivor *casts abilities with reckless abandon*, which makes killing
them close together the only sensible plan.

**Tempest Winds** accumulates: the winds *build over time, coalescing into tempest zones*, so
the usable floor shrinks the longer the fight runs. **Gale Force** pushes people, which is how
they end up in those zones.
