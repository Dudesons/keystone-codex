**No spells recorded in MDT, and 0.2 million health.**

This card used to rest on that second number. MDT gave the unit **21.6 million health — the
same as [Galvazzt](#/d/temple-of-sethraliss/codex/mob/263658) himself** — and a boss-sized health
bar on a spell-less NPC was the whole argument that it could not be decoration. **MDT 6.2.10
corrected the figure to 235,746.** The health bar was a data error, and the reasoning built on
it does not survive.

What survives is the half that never depended on it: Method's tracker lists **Lightning Spire**
among Galvazzt's abilities, categorised as party damage, avoidable, with a CC effect. So the
mechanic exists on one source's word rather than on two. Nothing is written into a spell list
here, because no matching ID is in the data.
