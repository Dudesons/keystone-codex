## What this pass did

Worked on 2026-08-25. Ticked boxes were acted on; unticked ones were read and are recorded below.

- **Severity 1, 3, 4, 5: nothing.** Severity 3 is empty *by construction* and that is confirmed
  rather than assumed: `## Interface: 120100` is identical in MDT 6.2.3's and 6.2.8's `.toc`, so
  no game patch landed between them and `FORCE=1 npm run fetch:assets` was deliberately not run.
- **Two mobs were renamed**, and `npm run scaffold` created a *second* card for each — cards are
  keyed by the `npcId:` field, not the filename, so the empty stub could have silently replaced
  the written card depending on glob order. Stubs discarded, `name:` applied to the real cards,
  and both pairs renamed (base and `.fr.md`) so scaffold stops recreating them. It now reports
  `0 files created`.
- **Five mobs newly flagged `isBoss` by MDT.** Four already carried `rank: miniboss`, so nothing
  moved for them. The fifth, T'zala, had no rank and therefore became **King's Rest's seventh
  boss** — caught by `highlights.test.ts`, not by reading. `_dungeon.md` names six and omits it,
  and its own card says it is King Dazar's mount fought alongside him: same shape as Echo of
  Nalorakk, so it is `rank: miniboss` now. The test passes as written rather than being retargeted.
- **20 pack-grouping findings, and the `packs:` trap did not fire.** One real card scopes a tip to
  a pack — the-blinding-vale's Sporeblight Belcher, `packs: [44]`. Pack 44's membership was
  compared before and after and is identical, so the tip still points where it says.
- **Maps: 0 rebuilt, 8 left alone**, and that is not a stale map. No `textureFolder` moved, and
  all **1200 tiles are byte-identical to the 6.2.3 tag**, compared by git blob SHA against
  `Nnoggie/MythicDungeonTools`.
- **Severity 2 (4 findings, altar-of-fangs) deliberately deferred.** All four predate this update:
  none of those four mobs gained or lost a single spell between 6.2.3 and 6.2.8. Annotating them
  is game knowledge, so it stays codex work rather than update work.

  **Amended after #32: three of those four findings were noise.** Severity 2 counted spell ids,
  and WoW gives one ability several — so an ability annotated once reported as several gaps. Of
  the 30 ids these four findings named, 22 were duplicates of an already-annotated ability, and
  Rav'i, Twinfang Harrower and Ritual Chieftain are in fact **complete**. The real remainder is
  one card: **259446 The Writhing Coil**, missing notes for Spiteful Hunt, Uncoiled, Assimilation,
  Spiteful Venom, Undermining, Toxic Barrage and Toxic Atrophy. Re-running the report on the
  fixed audit prints exactly that one finding. This section is left as it was generated rather
  than regenerated, because its severity 6 was measured against a base revision that has since
  moved; the deferral above still stands, over seven abilities on one card instead of thirty ids
  on four.
- **`dungeons.json` boss counts are now inflated** — King's Rest 6→7, Ruby Life Pools 4→8 — because
  that count comes from MDT's `isBoss` at extraction and cannot see a card's `rank`. The briefing
  is right; the home page card is not. Pre-existing (Den of Nalorakk already reads 4 against 3) and
  raised separately rather than fixed here.
